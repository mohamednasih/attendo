About these numbers

42 Intra provides daily location totals in locations_stats.json. Xattendo combines screen-lock intervals from /run/42-lock/seat0.json with saved workstation history and counts overlapping intervals once. Unobserved locks may be missing.

Attendo’s recorded hours already exclude locks. Reports update when you refresh.
